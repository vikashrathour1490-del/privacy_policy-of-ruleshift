The idea behind the game

A small puzzle with a bigger purpose.

Rule Shift was built to make focused thinking feel like a game—not a chore.

Why Rule Shift was created

The developer has struggled with overthinking for a long time and noticed that it could make it harder to stay focused and think clearly. That personal experience inspired the idea for Rule Shift: a simple puzzle game that invites players to pause, spot patterns, adapt to changing rules, and work through one challenge at a time.

The goal is to offer an enjoyable way to practise attention, flexible thinking and problem-solving. The game is not a medical treatment and does not claim to diagnose or cure any mental-health condition.

“One puzzle at a time. Pause, notice the rule, and find your next move.”

Runs on your phone

The core puzzle gameplay and level progress are designed to run locally on your device. Rule Shift does not need a constant internet connection for core gameplay.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026. This policy applies to the Rule Shift mobile game.

🛡️

At a glance: core gameplay is designed to work locally and offline. Game progress is stored on your device. The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to help identify crashes and technical issues; crash diagnostics may be transmitted to Google when a network connection is available.

01

Information handled by the app

Game progress: the current level and related progress are used to operate the game and are stored locally on your device in the current design.

Crash diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics may process crash logs, stack traces, app version, operating-system/device details and other technical diagnostics needed to understand a crash.

This wording assumes no separate analytics, account, cloud-sync or advertising SDK is enabled. Update this policy if your app includes one.

02

What we use it for

  • To keep the puzzle game and level progress working.
  • To detect, diagnose and fix app crashes or bugs.
  • To improve stability and performance.

We do not use crash diagnostics to advertise to you or to build a profile of your interests.

03

Local storage & offline play

The core game does not require an internet connection. Game progress is intended to remain on the phone rather than being uploaded to a Rule Shift server.

If you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, locally saved progress may be deleted. Device backup behaviour can vary by Android settings.

Crash reporting is different: Crashlytics may send technical crash data when the device can connect to the internet.

04

Firebase Crashlytics

Rule Shift uses Firebase Crashlytics, a crash-reporting service from Google. It helps the developer understand whether the app is crashing and which technical issue needs attention.

Crash reports may contain technical identifiers and device/app information. Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy documentation.

Firebase privacy and security information ↗

05

Information we do not ask for

The core puzzle gameplay is not designed to ask for your contacts, SMS/messages, personal photos, microphone recordings or precise location.

Crash diagnostics can still include technical device/app details. Android may also show standard system-level information or permissions depending on your device and installed app version.

06

Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We use Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics, which means relevant technical data may be processed by Google to provide that service.

We do not operate our own server to receive game-level progress in the current design. If a later version adds online accounts, cloud saves, analytics or ads, this policy should be updated before that version is released.

07

Retention & deletion

Game progress is stored locally and can generally be removed by clearing Rule Shift's app data or uninstalling the app, subject to Android backup settings.

Crash-report retention and processing are governed in part by Firebase settings and Google's service terms. For a privacy question or request, contact us using the email below.

08

Security

We aim to keep the app's data handling limited to what is needed for gameplay and crash diagnosis. No app, transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

09

Children's privacy

Rule Shift is not designed to knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe personal information has been submitted to us, contact us so we can review the concern.

Make sure the app's target audience and Play Console age settings are consistent with this policy.

10

Policy changes

This policy may change when the game, permissions or third-party services change. The latest version will be published at this page, with the updated date shown near the top.
