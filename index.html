1. Information We Collect

Rule Shift may collect information you provide directly, including account details, feedback, and support requests. We also collect limited technical information (such as device type, browser, and app performance metrics) needed to operate and improve the service.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve Rule Shift services.
  • Personalize product experience and settings.
  • Respond to support requests and service communications.
  • Protect against abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only when required to operate the service (for example, with trusted processors), to comply with legal obligations, or to protect user safety and service integrity.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal requirements.

5. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is fully secure, so we continually review and improve our controls.

6. Children's Privacy

Rule Shift is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided information to us, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing activities.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised version with an updated effective date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@ruleshift.app.
